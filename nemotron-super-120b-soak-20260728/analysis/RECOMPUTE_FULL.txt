## 1. COUNTER RESET CHECK (monotonicity)
All 6 spec_decode counters strictly non-decreasing across 147 scrapes.
NO RESET OCCURRED during the run. Every delta is a real delta.

Note on method: a reset corrupts a delta REGARDLESS of whether the window
boundary is defined by wall clock or by request count. Reset-immunity comes
from DETECTING the monotonicity violation (this check), not from the choice
of boundary. Fixed request-count windows buy comparability of window SIZE,
which is a real and separate benefit.

## 3. PER-FAMILY ACCEPTANCE - IS IT COMPUTABLE FROM THIS LOG?
Engine spec_decode counters are PROCESS-WIDE cumulative. They carry no
family label. Attribution needs a window containing only one family.

Family composition of each inter-scrape window (first 4 shown):
   {'code': 2, 'math': 2, 'prose': 2, 'reasoning': 2, 'structured': 2, 'summarize': 2, 'tool': 2}
   {'code': 2, 'math': 2, 'prose': 2, 'reasoning': 2, 'structured': 2, 'summarize': 2, 'tool': 2}
   {'code': 2, 'math': 2, 'prose': 2, 'reasoning': 2, 'structured': 2, 'summarize': 2, 'tool': 2}
   {'code': 2, 'math': 2, 'prose': 2, 'reasoning': 2, 'structured': 2, 'summarize': 2, 'tool': 2}

Every full window contains all 7 families in EQUAL counts: True

VERDICT: per-family acceptance is NOT computable from this log, and no
arithmetic on it can recover the split. The workload is round-robin one turn per
family and the scrape fires every 14 turns = exactly 2 complete cycles, so every
window is perfectly balanced by construction. The very design choice that makes
the DRIFT result strong - no family can occupy a contiguous stretch - is what
destroys family attribution in a process-wide counter.

This is a real limitation of the run, not something to paper over with a
derived estimate. Reporting a fabricated per-family split here would be worse
than reporting none.
